# Website Material Rights Review

Reviewed: 20 October 2026. This is a technical inventory and risk-reduction
record, not a legal opinion, assurance of non-infringement, or guarantee against
claims. No website can prevent another person from bringing a claim.

## Changes Made

- Kept the company images and partner logos based on the owner's explicit
  confirmation that permission exists for all of them.
- Self-hosted the unmodified Outfit font with its complete SIL OFL notice.
- Self-hosted Font Awesome Free's solid font and CSS with the complete original
  licence notices and copyright headers. No Pro files are used.
- Removed Google Fonts and Cloudflare CDN requests from the public application.
- Retained the self-hosted OpenStreetMap map, its ODbL source database, linked
  on-map credit and source licensing notice.
- Increased attribution text size and contrast, including on mobile.
- Added a visitor-accessible Credits & Privacy page, source database download,
  licence links, rights contact and qualified third-party ownership notice.
- Added a same-origin resource policy to stop automatic loading of new remote
  scripts, fonts, styles, images and API requests through this page.
- Added an asset check for missing files, missing notices and remote asset
  dependencies. It is not a legal-compliance test.

## Owner Records To Retain

Keep signed licences, releases or other evidence supporting the permission
confirmation, including the right to publish and self-host each image/logo on a
commercial website. Check territory, term, permitted modifications, partner
brand guidelines, revocation conditions, any required attribution, identifiable
people, and third-party material depicted within images. No permission documents
were supplied to or independently checked by this review.

Partner names were supplied by the owner. Relationship contracts, the scope of
endorsements, and ownership/registration of the INDR EV name and logo have not
been independently verified. A notice does not create a missing permission.

## Business And Legal Matters Outside Asset Clearance

The site's existing certification, government subsidy, revenue, warranty and
performance statements were not substantiated by documents in this workspace.
Examples requiring owner and professional review include ARAI/BIS/CEA/ISO claims,
99.93% accuracy, 70% FAME-linked support, 560 kW output, up to 1.5 lakh/month, and
the "10-Year Lifetime Warranty" wording. This update does not certify those
statements, change the contracts they may require, or guarantee eligibility for
any government programme. Generic notices do not cure a misleading claim.

The privacy notice describes the current front-end code and identifies the
hosting provider. The owner's broader handling of email enquiries, personal
data, retention, customer contracts and regulatory obligations needs a separate
review based on actual business practices and applicable law.

The pre-existing application code was supplied in this repository. Its author
and the source of the original marketing copy have not been independently
established. Keep the relevant developer agreements and content rights records.

Obtain a qualified lawyer's review of permissions, business statements and
applicable law before treating the website as legally cleared. Do not advertise
this website as "sue-free" or guaranteed compliant.

## Maintenance

Run `node scripts/check-asset-licences.cjs` after changing assets or dependencies.
New images, embeds, tracking tools, fonts, plugins, scripts or partner marks need
a new review. Preserve the vendor notices and OpenStreetMap attribution. Update
the privacy notice when data handling or external integrations change.
